Finance admin

Use this on a computer, about once a month. The tablet only displays the result.

1 · Import a bank CSV

Download the transactions CSV from your bank's website, then choose it here. Importing the same file twice is safe: lines already imported are skipped.



2 · Monthly budgets

The amount you plan to spend per category each month. Leave empty or 0 for no budget. Each box saves when you leave it.

3 · Fix categories

Your most frequent merchants over the last 90 days. Change a category once and every transaction from that merchant is updated; future imports remember it. Card payments and transfers between your own accounts should be "Transfers" so they don't count as spending.
